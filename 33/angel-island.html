Angel Island Campout

Overnight Expedition · Summer Solstice

Dates
Sun, June 21 – Mon, June 22
Depart SF
2:05 PM Ferry
SF Ferry Building, Gate B
Campground
North Garrison Group
Capacity
30 people

We’re camping out overnight on Angel Island to celebrate Dixon’s Trenta-y-Tresiñera and the summer solstice. We can accommodate up to 30 people in the North Garrison Group campground.

Bring WARM layers! It gets cold on the island at night, even in June. Bay Area fog is no joke.
⛺

Itinerary

  • 2:05 PM Depart SF Ferry Building, Gate B
  • ~3:00 PM Arrive Angel Island
  • ~3:30 PM Set up camp + shenanigans
  • 4:50 PM Last ferry off the island (for anyone not staying overnight)
  • 8:35 PM Sunset — incredible views of the city and the Bay
  • More shenanigans · sleep(?)

Monday Morning Ferries

  • 10:10 AM — first ferry departs Angel Island; arrives SF 10:40 AM
  • 11:35 AM — second ferry departs Angel Island
  • 12:20 PM — second ferry arrives SF
Yes, it’s a Sunday–Monday — weekend reservations are hard to get. But you can be back in SF by 10:40 AM Monday if you take the first ferry off the island.
